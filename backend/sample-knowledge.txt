Jhinku AI — Company Knowledge Base
==================================

About Us
--------
Jhinku AI is an AI-powered customer support platform that helps
businesses answer customer questions instantly using their own
knowledge base. Companies upload FAQs and documents, and Jhinku AI
trains a chatbot that answers on their website 24/7.

Pricing
-------
We offer three plans:

- Starter (Free): 1 chatbot, up to 100 AI messages per month,
  5 knowledge documents.
- Growth ($29/month): 3 chatbots, 5,000 AI messages per month,
  unlimited documents, branding customization.
- Business ($99/month): unlimited chatbots and messages,
  priority support, human handoff, API access, and analytics.

All paid plans include a 14-day free trial. No credit card is
required to start the trial.

Getting Started
---------------
1. Create an account at app.jhinku.ai
2. Create your organization and invite your team.
3. Create a chatbot and upload your FAQs or documents.
4. Copy the widget code and paste it before </body> on your website.
5. Test the chatbot, then go live.

The widget code looks like this:
<script src="https://api.jhinku.ai/widget.js"
        data-chatbot-id="YOUR_PUBLIC_WIDGET_ID" async></script>

Common Questions
----------------
Q: Can I use my own branding?
A: Yes. Growth and Business plans support custom colors, logo, and
welcome messages for the chat widget.

Q: Does the AI make up answers?
A: No. The AI only answers using your uploaded documents. If it
cannot find relevant information, it offers to connect the customer
to a human agent instead of guessing.

Q: What file types can I upload?
A: PDF, TXT, and DOCX files up to 10 MB each.

Q: Can I have multiple chatbots?
A: Yes. Growth plans include 3 chatbots and Business plans include
unlimited chatbots. Each chatbot can have its own knowledge base.

Q: How do I update my knowledge base?
A: Go to Knowledge Base in your dashboard, upload new documents, or
delete old ones. The AI automatically re-indexes when documents
change.
